Adding or removing an addon saves to the TV right away. For the other boxes, tap Save to TV. Changes are sent to your TV over the internet, encrypted end to end. Jarvis must be open on the TV to receive them, or they apply the next time it opens (within 12 hours).

    This link

    Anyone with this link can change your TV's setup. Make a new link on the TV to cut off old devices. Page version 2026-11-23.1.

    Your other TVs

    Every TV in your house, with its name, platform, Jarvis version and when it was last seen. Find my TVs asks them to report in (about 10 seconds). Open switches this page to that TV. Copy once gives another TV (a friend's, say) a one-time copy of your setup without your Real-Debrid, and the TVs don't stay linked. TVs that share settings stay the same: change a key, an addon or the playlist on one, and the others update within seconds. Watch history and Continue Watching stay separate on each TV.
